The Hill Institute

for Innovation and Entrepreneurship is a nationally awarded institute that develops innovative and entrepreneurially minded leaders who are equipped to transform organizations, our communities, and the world.  This mission is carried out through best-in-class experiential learning programs, pitch competitions, new venture accelerators, and thought leadership forums.

We offer a diverse range of programs for both active Furman students (undergraduate and graduate) and non-Furman founders in the community.

At The Hill Institute, we believe in nurturing the entrepreneurial and creative spirit within our community, empowering individuals to transform their innovative ideas into real-world ventures.

Furman’s Next Big Idea, with its focus on ideation, serves as a catalyst for students to develop new ideas and problem-based solutions. Meanwhile, the Rinker Paladin Pitch Competition and GVL Starts programs support current and future founders through weekly training, mentorship, and access to potential grant funding.

Our Clemens Angel Analyst Fellowship—one of the few programs in the nation designed specifically for undergraduate students—offers Furman students an incredible opportunity to explore the world of venture capital alongside industry leaders at VentureSouth. We have also launched the Innovation Academy, expanding our reach into high school programming.

At The Hill Institute, our programming is designed to develop entrepreneurial leaders through education, mentorship, and hands-on experience. Beyond our programs, we host thought leadership forums, pitch competitions, and launch parties—events that enhance learning, foster connections, and extend our impact into the Greenville community. Covering topics from venture capitalism to quantum mechanics and AI, these events add depth and excitement to the innovation ecosystem.
